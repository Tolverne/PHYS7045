# Exam preparation

- `formula_sheets/` — concise equations, assumptions and notation.
- `topic_summaries/` — one- or two-page revision sheets.
- `practice_questions/` — exam-style questions, preferably grouped by topic.
- `worked_past_exams/<year>/` — **your worked answers**, not original exam papers.
- `mistakes_and_gaps/` — misunderstandings and common errors to revisit.

Keep original exam PDFs under `../source_material/past_exams/<year>/`.
