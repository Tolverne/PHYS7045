Question 2. 
Consider a pair of trapped ions, that will share collective phonon modes. The ions are 
prepared initially in the collective motional ground state. The following 3-pulse sequence is 
applied to the ions to realise an entangling gate between the two ions.  
1. Red side band 𝜋-pulse on ion A that couples the ground state |𝑔⟩ and the excited 
electronic state |𝑒⟩. 
2. A red sideband 2𝜋-pulse applied to ion B that couples to an |𝑔⟩ to an auxiliary excited 
state |𝑎⟩. 
3. A red sideband 𝜋-pulse applied ion A coupling the ground state |𝑔⟩ and the excited 
electronic state |𝑒⟩. 
 
Part A (grade: 1-5) 
1. Determine the action of step 1 of the gate to the basis states of the two-qubit system, 
|𝑔𝑔⟩, |𝑔𝑒⟩, |𝑒𝑔⟩, |𝑒𝑒⟩. 
2. Starting from the result of step 1, determine the action of step 2. 
3. Starting from the result of step 2, determine the action of step 3. 
4. Write down the resulting 4x4 matrix for this gate and identify its action. 
5. Describe how one could realise a CNOT gate using this entangling gate along with single 
qubit gates (you do not need to calculate the result of the circuit). 
 
Part B (Advanced: grade +0,+1,+2)  
Now consider three qubits labelled 𝐴, 𝐵, 𝐶. Consider how the sequence can be modified to 
extend the gate to a controlled-controlled gate architecture. 
a) Write down the mapping of the qubit states |𝐴𝐵𝐶⟩*+*,*-. →|𝐴𝐵𝐶⟩/*+-. for the controlled-
controlled operation. 
 
b) Determine a modification that realises the operation by adding an additional two pulses 
to the sequence.

In this protocol, Alice and Bob share in advance an entangled qubit pair in the |𝜓0⟩ state, i.e. 
one part of the pair is with Alice, and the other part is with Bob. The goal is to remotely 
prepare the state  |𝛽⟩= (|0⟩+ 𝑒*1|1⟩)/√2  on Bob’s side with Alice sending just one bit to 
Bob.   To do this, Alice applies to her qubit a Bloch sphere rotation by 𝜋/2 about the axis 𝑛A =
(−sin 𝜑, cos 𝜑, 0).  Then she performs a measurement in the computational basis and sends 
the result to Bob.  
 
Part A (grade: 1-5) 
1. Show that the state of Bob’s qubit after Alice does a measurement is (up to a global 
phase factor) either |𝛽⟩= (|0⟩+ 𝑒*1|1⟩)/√2  or |𝛽2⟩= (|0⟩−𝑒*1|1⟩)/√2.  
2. Draw and describe an experimental setup that uses polarisation-entangled photons to 
achieve the remote state preparation described above. 
3. The remote state preparation protocol described above is constrained in that the 
quantum state given by |𝛽⟩ lies along the equator of the Bloch sphere (i.e. |𝛽⟩ is an 
equatorial state). Consider a state |𝛽′⟩ which is an arbitrary point anywhere on the Bloch 
sphere. How would the experimental setup you drew for A.2 change to implement the 
remote state preparation of |𝛽′⟩? 
4. Compare the remote state preparation to the quantum teleportation protocol. Discuss 
similarities and differences. 
 
Part B (Advanced: grade +0,+1,+2)  
Although quantum teleportation is often done with photons, it is also possible to do quantum 
teleportation using continuous variables (CV) of the electromagnetic field. Discuss 
advantages and disadvantages of using CV for quantum teleportation.
