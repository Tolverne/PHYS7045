# Topic-based study notes

Write and refine explanations in the topic folders here; leave original source PDFs and transcripts in `../source_material/`.

The topic grouping is provisional and may be adjusted after identifying lecture coverage and high-frequency past-exam questions.

For each major topic, aim for: key ideas, definitions, a small number of fully explained derivations, a concrete worked example, and links back to the relevant lecture/transcript or assignment question.
